Your Profile page is the central place to manage your personal account details and brand identity. Navigate to it at any time by clicking your account menu and selecting **Profile**. From here you can update your contact information, change your password, upload a custom logo, and — if you ever need to — permanently delete your account.

## Profile Information

Keep your display name and email address up to date so your account stays accessible and recognizable across the platform.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your Profile page">
    Log in to the VAMS admin interface and navigate to **Profile** from the main navigation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit your name and email">
    Locate the **Profile Information** section. Enter your updated **Name** and **Email** in the respective fields.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your changes">
    Click **Save**. A brief confirmation message will appear once your profile has been updated successfully.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  If you change your email address, your account will be marked as unverified. A new verification link will be sent to your updated address — check your inbox and click the link to re-verify before continuing.
</Note>

## Changing Your Password

You can update your password at any time from the **Update Password** section on the Profile page.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Update Password">
    Scroll down the Profile page to the **Update Password** section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your current and new password">
    Fill in your **Current Password**, then enter and confirm your **New Password**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your new password">
    Click **Save**. Your password will be updated immediately and all future logins will require the new password.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Choose a strong, unique password and store it in a password manager. Your password is never shown again after it is set.
</Tip>

## Uploading Your Brand Logo

Your brand logo appears in the navigation bar of the VAMS admin interface and can be used to reinforce your identity across the platform.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the Custom Logo section">
    At the top of the Profile page, find the **Custom Logo** section. If you have no logo uploaded yet, a placeholder image icon is shown.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a logo file">
    Click **Select Logo** and choose an image file from your device. Any common image format is accepted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload the logo">
    Once a file is selected, its filename will appear below the button. Click **Upload** to apply it. The navigation bar will refresh to display your new logo.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Replace or remove the logo (optional)">
    To swap your logo, select a new file and click **Upload** again. To remove your logo entirely, click **Remove Logo** — the interface will revert to the default VAMS branding.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Your logo is stored securely and served via a CDN-backed URL. There is no maximum file size enforced in the interface, but keeping your logo under 1 MB ensures fast load times.
</Note>

## Deleting Your Account

If you need to permanently close your VAMS account, the **Delete Account** section is located at the bottom of the Profile page.

<Warning>
  **This action is permanent and cannot be undone.** Deleting your account removes all of your data — including your albums, mosaics, entries, media files, and API keys — immediately and irreversibly. There is no recovery option after deletion. Make sure you have exported anything you need before proceeding.
</Warning>

To delete your account, click **Delete Account**, confirm your current password when prompted, and follow the confirmation steps. You will be logged out and redirected to the home page once deletion is complete.
